Transatlantic Math and Cultural Exchange: U.S. Experience for Ukrainian Future Students

The Transatlantic Academic and Cultural Exchange brings together Ukrainian and U.S. students for online collaboration. The program is designed to support mutual learning about Ukraine and the United States while improving English-language communication and mathematics skills.

This program is presented by Mr. Kenneth Bogle, a seasoned American educator with more than 40 years of teaching experience in mathematics and computer science. Mr. Bogle has served as a K–12 Instructional Computer Coordinator and has taught Advanced Placement (AP) Computer Science and AP Calculus. He has also developed curricula for several computer science courses.

He holds two master’s degrees from Lehigh University, one in Statistical Research in Education and one in Educational Technology, as well as several certificates in coding and computer science. Known for his dedication and passion for teaching, Mr. Bogle brings extensive knowledge and a valuable international perspective to this program.
